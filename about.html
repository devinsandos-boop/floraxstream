Our story

A flower shop with a soft spot
for everyday magic.

We believe flowers are never “just flowers.” They are a pause, a thank you, a celebration, or a way to say I’m here.

How we started

A studio full of stems & possibility.

Flora & Stem began with a market bucket of peonies and a small dream: make it easier to send someone a little joy. Today, our neighborhood studio is still guided by that same bright idea.

We work with local growers whenever we can, follow the seasons, and favour arrangements that feel artful, generous and genuinely alive.

Our promiseEvery arrangement is designed by hand, not assembled on a conveyor belt. Your flowers will be as individual as the person receiving them.
What matters to us

Good flowers, good people.

Seasonal firstWe select flowers in their natural moment — beautiful, long-lasting and full of character.
Thoughtfully localWe nurture relationships with local growers and small makers in our community.
Less waste, more joyWe compost our green waste and use recyclable packaging whenever possible.
